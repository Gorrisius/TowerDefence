BLOODROOT - FREE VERSION
Version 1.0

FREE VERSION
  Stand, Idle and Walk in 8 directions.
  The FULL version adds Spike Burst, Ranged Attack, Roar and Death.

SPECS
  Canvas:      112x112 px per frame, transparent background
  Character:   approx. 60x60 px, ground line at y=96, centre at x=56 in every frame
  View:        low top-down (3/4)
  Directions:  south, south-east, east, north-east, north, north-west, west, south-west
  Frames:      frame_00.png ... in play order, 9 frames per animation, suggested 10 fps

SHADOWS
  Shadows/ holds a shadow-only layer for every frame (draw it under the sprite);
  WithShadow/ holds every frame with the shadow already applied.
